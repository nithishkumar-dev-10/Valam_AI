## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Valam AI is a voice-first agriculture assistant built for Indian farmers. Ask a question the way you'd ask a field expert — in Tamil or English — by speaking, by taking a photo of your crop, or by sharing your location. Valam answers instantly, in your language, with an easy-to-understand recommendation you can play aloud.

WHAT IT CAN DO
• Talk to it. Record a question in Tamil or English. Valam transcribes it with on-server speech recognition and replies with a spoken answer.
• Crop recommendation. Share your location and Valam suggests a crop suited to your land, using your area's weather and rainfall data.
• Disease detection. Snap a leaf or fruit and Valam identifies 38 common plant diseases with a confidence score.
• Pest & weed detection. Upload a field photo and Valam identifies common agricultural pests and 9 major weed species, so you can target treatment.
• Voice answers. Results are spoken aloud in Tamil or English — made for the field, when your hands are busy.

MADE FOR THE FIELD
• Bilingual Tamil / English interface.
• Lightweight and designed for low-cost smartphones.
• No account needed — try every feature without signing up.
• No ads, no data selling.

PRIVACY
Your voice notes and photos are processed to answer your question and are not kept. See our full Privacy Policy at https://valam.in/privacy.

IMPORTANT
Valam AI provides informational guidance generated by software models. It is not a substitute for advice from an agronomist, extension officer, or plant-protection professional. Always verify before acting on any crop or plant treatment.
```
`~1500 chars` — well under the 4000 limit, room to grow.

## Claims discipline

Keep the description strictly true to the shipped MVP (ML + DL only):

  inference runs on the server; the app is a thin client.

  schemes, tutorials) — those endpoints do not exist.
- If a feature changes, update this file, `DATA_SAFETY_FORM.md`, and
