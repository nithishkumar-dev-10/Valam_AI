Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Valam AI ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Valam AI Android application, package in.valam.app (the "App"), and the optional web app. We built this App specifically for Indian farmers; we do not sell your personal data, and we collect only what is needed to provide agricultural guidance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account info: solely to authenticate you and deliver your account profile.
  • Voice recordings: transcribed to text so we can understand your question; the text (not the audio) is used to route your query to the right AI model.
  • Photos: analysed by our local AI models to identify plant diseases, weeds, or pests.
  • GPS coordinates: sent to the third-party services listed below to fetch weather and rainfall data for your area, which powers the crop recommendation feature.
  • Cookie: keep you signed in between sessions.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to anyone. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling. The only data transmissions to third parties are the service calls listed in Section 3, which are strictly necessary to provide the App's features.

5. Cookies and Tracking

The only cookie is the httpOnly refresh_token cookie described above, used solely to keep you signed in. We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies and no other web trackers.

7. Data Security

  • All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing — your plaintext password is never stored.
  • Authentication uses short-lived JWTs: the access token lasts 15 minutes and is held in memory only; the refresh token lasts 28 days but is rotated on every use and revoked server-side on logout, account deletion, and reuse detection.
  • The refresh token is delivered as an httpOnly + Secure + SameSite cookie and is never accessible to JavaScript or localStorage.
  • Photos are processed in memory and never written to disk; audio temp files are deleted immediately after transcription.
  • The Android build blocks all cleartext (plain HTTP) traffic at the operating-system level.
  • Our server is hosted on Oracle Cloud infrastructure with firewall rules restricting access.

8. Your Rights — Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app at any time: open the account screen and choose "Delete my account". Your account (name, phone number, and password hash) is permanently removed immediately, with no email request needed.

For completeness, you may also request deletion by emailing us at: privacy@valam.in

We will process an emailed deletion request within 30 days of verifying your identity (we will ask you to confirm via your registered phone number). Once deleted:

  • Your account, name, phone number, and password hash will be permanently removed.
  • Voice recordings and photos are never stored; generated TTS audio clips are automatically removed by the 30-day retention sweep and are not linked to your account.
  • Anonymised server logs (containing only IP addresses and timestamps, not linked to your account) may be retained for security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.
